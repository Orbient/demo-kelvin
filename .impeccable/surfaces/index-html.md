# Surface: index.html (landing page, React + Vite, src/App.jsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners of city flats and country houses near Moscow, and interior designers and architects, choosing light by how the evening will feel. Action: send the three-step request with its live estimate (posted to VITE_LEAD_WEBHOOK or a demo handler, a letter as the second way), set lamps aside in the product sheet, or visit the showroom. Proof: none of the client's own; stock photography and footage stand in for product shots (the collection is one cottonbro studio shoot on Pexels in a vintage lighting workshop, files `public/assets/img/w-*.jpg` with unlit versions in `img/off/`; the dark room is Annie Spratt / Unsplash; films from Pexels and Pixabay; all listed in `public/credits.html`). Constraints from the owner: Russian copy, with an English version (`src/lib/i18n.js`, `?lang=en`, USD at 90 ₽/$); a personalised demo (`src/lib/brand.js`, `?brand=Name` replaces the brand in the hero, header, footer, title and the logo's initial); no AI-site tells (the earlier ban on gradients was lifted when the owner asked for React Bits glows, gradients and animated lines, see OWN-WORLD); media only from stock platforms; use React Bits components; a stock video that plays as the visitor scrolls; maximum scroll motion; must read as a $20k studio build; no sound. The page ends on a «made by Orbient» card (`.made`). Owner notifications: `src/lib/track.js` sends open, leave and lead beacons to an n8n webhook (`VITE_TRACK_WEBHOOK`), silent on localhost unless `?track=1`; the n8n workflow «Kelvin demo · визиты и заявки» posts them to the owner's Telegram. v1 (static, in v1-static/) was rejected by the owner as looking AI-made. Any other path renders the 404 (`src/sections/NotFound.jsx` via `src/main.jsx`; the build copies index.html to dist/404.html).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The name is the unit of colour temperature, so the page is told as a scroll story in which light comes on: the visitor's scroll throws the switch, and every chapter is a lamp lit in front of them. Refuses the category default of a product carousel under a lifestyle hero.

OWN-WORLD: Soot black (#0d0c0b), lamp-shade ink (#ecebe8), filament amber (#ffd8a1) for what is lit (names, figures, whatever is switched on), one 2700 K accent (#ffad47) that fills only what the visitor switches, selects or sends (and is the light inside drawn light), dusk green (#141b18) for the dacha chapter, night (#070809) for the light map; the whole page is dark, the request form, the product sheet and the menu included (the plaster palette survives only as variable names redefined to light text tones inside `.request`). Oranienbaum (narrow, sharp Cyrillic display serif) at monument scale against Onest text; the name drawn as filament (FilamentText). Soft corners by the owner's request («квадратные кнопки — моветон»): pill buttons and chips, 20–28px panels. Flat fills and no drop shadows; no glass and no backdrop blur: panels are the flat Pane (rgba(18,16,14,.88) + 1px warm hairline), and the header bar `.hdr-glass` is flat too. Light is carried by footage, photographs and drawn light in filament and 2700 K, allowed only in its named places: StarBorder on the header call, FilamentText (hero name, footer name, 404 number) and the logo's filament initial, DappledLight behind the dacha heading, the process wire and bulbs, the map halos, the trade candela curve, the try-on glow, the dark-room torch, LightRays over the request, SideRays in the footer. The visitor holds the light: a page-wide Kelvin dimmer in the header (1800–4000 K, a fixed multiply layer `.kelvin-tint`, off at 2700 K). One veil of black (`.veil`, `useVeil` in App.jsx) dims softly at each seam between rooms.

STORY: The visitor understands the studio sells tuned light, believes it because the room switches on under their own scroll and they can switch, dim and re-tint lamps themselves (in the dark room, across the whole page, on a photo of their own room), then sees where the light already burns, how the work goes and what designers get, and sends a request with a live estimate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A loader first: a coiled filament draws and warms with the real download of the opening film, read out on an odometer 0 → 2700 K, then a greeting for the time of day and the curtain lifts. Then the dark room (a still of the film) with the name drawn as filament across the foot of the screen, one sentence of what this is above it on the left and the switch «Включить свет» on the right. Header: the bulb mark with the name (hidden on the first screen); past half a screen a flat soot bar settles in with the section nav (a filament pill that follows the active section), the RU/EN switch, the Kelvin dimmer and «Подобрать свет» as a StarBorder pill (a 2700 K comet circling a soot pill, in a Magnet); the StaggeredMenu toggle. Scrolling (or the switch, in place): "Вечер начинается с темноты." → a hand flips the switch → "Щёлк." → a readout climbs 0 → 2700 K as the filament catches → "Так горит каждая наша лампа" + CTA.

FORM: Scroll-triggered storytelling in chapters (ui-ux-pro-max pattern) on the taste-skill "name as monument" direction. Sections in order: Prologue (the switch-on room, a real film scrubbed by the scroll as its playhead, forwards and backwards; all-intra encoding, held in memory, one hardware seek in flight at a time so the page keeps 60 fps; the switch plays the same scene in place and hands the scroll back at the same moment); Manifesto (ScrollReveal lit in filament beside a frameless screened filament film that the scroll dims up); Chandelier «Мста» (a pinned locked-off film in a mask that opens to the screen with a counter-zoom, the spec and price on a Pane); Vitrine (`Vitrine.jsx`: eight lamps in the dark, only the centre one lit; a pinned horizontal row on desktop, a swipe row on phones, edges faded with a mask; replaced CircularGallery and CurvedLoop); Room (`Room.jsx`: four light sources switched on the photograph room-on.jpg / room-off.jpg, a torch cursor in the dark, dimmer and Kelvin on ElasticSliders, controls on a Pane); Dacha (`DuskFilm.jsx`: DappledLight sun through leaves behind the heading, a ScrollExpand still whose windows light one by one, then the film plays; the accordion gallery was removed); LightMap (`LightMap.jsx`: a night scheme of Moscow, projects light up); Process (`Process.jsx`: four steps on a filament wire with bulbs, pinned on desktop; replaced ScrollStack); Trade (`Trade.jsx`: for designers and architects, an animated polar candela curve, terms, files); Request (Stepper form on dark under a LightRays cone, three steps, a live estimate on a Pane; the old service tiles and the catalogue list were removed); footer (SideRays onto the filament name); «made by Orbient» end card. Product sheet (`ProductSheet.jsx`): FLIP flight from the pressed card, Kelvin chips tint the photo, finishes, «Примерить у себя» (try-on: a local photo or the sample room, the lamp screened on, dragged, sized by a slider), cart pill (`CartPill.jsx`). Logo (`src/components/Logo.jsx`): a bulb outline whose filament is the brand's initial; also the favicon. StaggeredMenu opens dark (no animated grain: it cost frame rate on every scene). Files in src/components/rb/ that are not imported (BorderGlow, SpotlightCard, CircularGallery, CurvedLoop, AccordionGallery, ScrollStack, StrokeText, Threads, FlowingMenu, MaskedHeading, ScrollVelocity, Noise, ImageTrail, BlurText, CountUp, SplitText) are not part of the system. Engineering: WebGL scenes (LightRays, SideRays, DappledLight) mount only through src/components/Lazy.jsx; every video plays only through playWhileOnScreen in src/lib/media.js (one at a time, off-screen films release their decoder; two playing together drop Chrome to 30 Hz for good); every SplitText call passes charsClass/wordsClass/linesClass so the .char-mask/.word-mask/.line-mask padding keeps glyphs whole, and masks inside headings take pointer-events: none (they once covered the hero switch); .star-border__light keeps max-width: none against the global canvas max-width; focus states and touch targets live in src/polish.css (a warm ring and halo only for keyboard focus, pills keep round focus, 44px targets on coarse pointers). Section heads: title and intro under it, left-aligned. Motion system from REA study of Lusion / Immersive Garden / Obys: curves lux (.35,0,0,1) and silk (.16,1,.3,1), words tip up in masked lines, running text surfaces line by line, the name draws as filament, lamps catch with a flicker, labels roll letter by letter, an odometer preloader, the veil at seams. Calls to action in the story are words with a drawn line; filled controls are the switches, selections and the request, and the header call is a soot pill lit by its comet. seed: skipped (owner instruction, not attempted). The impeccable concept-seed roll was deliberately skipped by the owner's instruction to use the skills «используя их инструкции, чтобы они не пересекались»: taste-skill owns art direction and its draw dealt the direction.
